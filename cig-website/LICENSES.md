## The 3D models

The cardiovascular model and the thoracic model currently shipped are both
**generated procedurally in code** (`src/lib/cardio3d/model.ts` and
`src/lib/cardio3d/thorax.ts`). They are original to this project and carry no
third-party licence obligations.

**If you replace it with an external model, record it here.** Several openly
licensed sources carry mandatory attribution — CC BY-SA in particular is
share-alike, meaning derivatives must be released under the same terms. See

## The bedside monitor, the patient room and the clinical simulation

The simulated cardiac monitor, the patient-room scene, the two-dimensional
patient models and the ECG waveforms are **original to this project**. The
monitor is a brand-neutral educational representation of the class of hospital
bedside monitors: its layout, colours, typography, labels and alarm behaviour are
CIG's own, and it deliberately reproduces no manufacturer's interface, branding,
icon set or product design. It is labelled in the interface as a teaching
simulation of no real patient.

The male and female patient models used by the Clinical Simulation are generated
in code from a table of body half-widths (`src/components/simulation/TorsoStage.tsx`)
— no photograph, medical illustration or scanned anatomy is used, so there is no
third-party image licence to observe. Every clinical case in
`content/simulation/cases/` is fictional and written for CIG; none describes,
paraphrases or is derived from a real patient record.

## Audio

There are no audio files in this repository. Every heart sound and murmur is
**synthesised in the browser** by `src/lib/audio/heartSounds.ts` from the timing
and frequency model in `content/exam/heart-sounds.json`, and the monitor tones
are generated by `src/lib/ecg/monitorTone.ts`. Nothing is sampled from a
recording, so there is no third-party audio licence to observe.

If CIG later adds real auscultation recordings, they must be properly licensed
and recorded with consent, and the source and licence recorded here.

citation in `content/references.json` links to the publisher's page; those
publications remain the copyright of their publishers and are cited, not
